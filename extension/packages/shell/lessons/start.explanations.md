The side panel has four tabs:

- **Advice**: the day's orders, once the pages they need have been read. Under them come any
  order the limits cut or stopped, what stopped buying, what is missing and the strategy's
  warnings, each in a short sentence. "Read this page" reads the broker page in front of you,
  and a page you saved can be pasted in or opened from a file.
- **Settings**: the strategy and its settings, the income goal, the risk limits, your broker's
  fee preset, the stocks to choose from, the stocks to leave out, and any deposit or withdrawal
  you type in.
- **History**: what has been recorded for each stock, and "Forget everything".
- **Lessons**: this course, module by module.

What the panel works out never depends on how much of the course you have read. The notice at
the foot of every tab says what steadyhand is: example software whose choices and orders are
yours.
