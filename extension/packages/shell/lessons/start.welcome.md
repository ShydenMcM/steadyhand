**What it is.** steadyhand is a browser extension. Its side panel reads the pages your broker's
website shows you, such as your portfolio and your watchlist, and works out what the strategy
you chose implies for the day: which stocks to buy or sell, how many lots, and at what limit
price. It is built for one goal in particular: growing a portfolio's dividend income over many
years.

**What it is not.** It is not a service, a broker or an adviser. It sends nothing anywhere:
what it reads and records stays in your browser, and "Forget everything" on the History tab
deletes it. Its strategies are examples that show how a set of rules behaves.

**It never trades for you.** It only reads your broker's pages, and changes nothing on them. You
place every order yourself, in your broker's own app or website, or none at all.

**You can lose money.** Share prices fall, sometimes a long way and for a long time. Companies
cut their dividends. A strategy that did well in the past can do badly next. Only invest money
you can leave alone through a bad stretch.

The lessons that follow explain the ideas behind every number the panel shows, one at a time.
